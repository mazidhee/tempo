## Selection-round override and quality bar

This is an owner-directed options round, not a randomly selected implementation
concept. No concept roll or seed was used: the owner explicitly requested five
compositions for every screen and will make the selection. Code previews are the
decision artifacts; there is no approved comp yet. The official Linear board/search
references and the owner's Things screenshots calibrate the requested operating
quality, not a certified ceiling. No catalog QUALITY BAR card was supplied, and
this artifact must not claim a 9.9 finish or catalog ceiling. After selection,
implementation needs its own approved contract and full finish review.

For this decision tool, the working bar is: one clear workspace query field;
all scheduled times appear in every Plan option; habit names and check-ins remain
visible; five genuinely different structures per screen; charts state their units
and keep axis labels readable; no private data or app database access.

## Direction contract

THESIS: quiet, immediately usable daily operations. Every label earns its place;
habits never disappear inside time-of-day headings and recovery has its own destination.

OWN-WORLD: retain Tempo's original waveform, solid surfaces, existing accent freedom
and Inter typography. In the options, graphite and paper share one teal accent.
Controls have explicit focus states; search has a defined field at the top. New-item
actions are unboxed text controls. No gradients or decorative copy.

STORY: manage tasks on a Kanban board, set a scheduled date/time, then work from Plan
without re-entering the same task. Direct habit check-ins lead to dated history and
trends. Work projects lead directly to milestones and project tasks.

FIRST VIEWPORT: narrow app navigation, a bounded top search field, a short page title,
one primary action and the working content. Design-studio controls sit outside the
mock app. All habit names and check-ins are visible without expanding a section.

FORM: five intentionally different structures per screen, all shown because the
owner explicitly requested five rather than a three-option concept round. Workflow,
schedule, focus, project lanes and compact queue boards keep urgency independent of
workflow status. Supporting screens vary their topology, not just colours.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish
review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
This options artifact does not approve a new app-wide design system or change DESIGN.md.

## Scope and protection

Options cover Tasks, Plan, Habits, History, Recovery, Work, Focus, Search, Home,
Study, Statistics, Goals, Journal, Review and Settings (75 screen compositions).
Fictional data only. Selection is stored only in the prototype's browser storage.
Existing app source and owner data are untouched. No evening reflection in Plan;
Journal and Review remain separate. Essential action labels, errors and accessible
names must survive any copy reduction. No task or habit feature is silently deleted.

The owner confirmed they imported their own Rehab CSV. Do not run the pending owner
import script or re-import it. The 1.7 source/build checkpoint remains recoverable;
the installed all-record gate was not completed and must not be presented as passed.

## Reference research

- [Linear search](https://linear.app/docs/search): bounded search, scoped results,
  recent items and keyboard access. We choose a permanently visible top field for
  this owner, rather than adopting Linear's hidden shortcut entry point.
- [Linear boards](https://linear.app/docs/board-layout): workflow columns, inline
  column creation, project grouping and moving cards without dragging via keyboard.
- [Things](https://culturedcode.com/things/): the owner's requested benchmark for
  a calm daily workspace. Original compositions and assets only; no copied product code.

The prototype is a decision tool. Real persistence, migrations, drag accessibility,
automatic Plan scheduling and release acceptance belong to the implementation after
the owner chooses. No mock chart represents the owner's data.
